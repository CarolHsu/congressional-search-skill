# Required Output Structure

Use exactly these 13 sections, in order. Tables wherever they help scanning. Concise.

Open with one header line: member, chamber, audience mode, packet files used, `STATUS CHECKED: <date>`.

## 1. MEMBER SNAPSHOT
Table: name; state/district; party; committees; relevant subcommittees; committee leadership; congressional leadership; relevant caucuses (each tagged DIRECT RELEVANCE or STRATEGIC CONNECTION); Congressional Taiwan Caucus membership (only if VERIFIED, else NEEDS RECHECK); relevant background; district industries; recurring policy interests.

## 2. TAIWAN / CHINA RECORD
Votes, sponsored and cosponsored legislation, statements, caucus participation, defense/trade/tech positions, Taiwan visits. Recent first. End with the A–E classification and a 2–3 sentence rationale.

## 3. FULL BILL MATRIX
One row per in-scope priority from the supplied packet:

| Issue | Bill | Current Status | Committee | Member on Committee? | Sponsor/Cosponsor? | Relevant Past Vote? | Member Fit | Actionability |

If a standalone bill's language rides on another vehicle, show both ("Standalone: H.R. XXXX / More advanced vehicle: H.R. YYYY") and say which one matters legislatively. Where the verified status differs from the packet, mark it.

## 4. TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS
The three chosen issues with the scoring logic and portfolio role (high-probability / high-leverage / strategic expansion).

## 5. CTA MATRIX
Most important table; hand-off ready for the Advocacy Captain.

| Priority | Bill | Legislative Position | Member Position | PRIMARY CTA | Secondary CTA |

Beneath it, per issue: STRETCH CTA and DO-NOT-ASK. In `staff` mode, phrase CTAs as "recommend the Member…" and add the mechanics (contact, deadline, lead office).

## 6. WHY THESE 3 / WHY NOT THE OTHERS
Per selected issue: WHY THIS MEMBER, labeled VERIFIED CONNECTION or STRATEGIC INFERENCE. Then one line per deprioritized issue.

## 7. RELATIONSHIP HOOKS
- `staff` mode: who likely covers the portfolio (NEEDS RECHECK if not verified), one authentic constituent/local tie as the reason for the meeting, and what makes the ask easy for staff to carry back. Skip personal small talk.
- `member` mode: authentic connections from the delegation (constituent, district residence, employer, university, industry, community, immigration experience, background), who should introduce the group, and the bridge: connection → member interest → Taiwan → bill → CTA.
- Never open with an apology ("we're not from your district"). Never manufacture a connection; if none exists, say so.

## 8. TALKING POINTS
Per selected issue, only: ONE-SENTENCE FRAMING; 2–3 KEY FACTS; WHY THIS MEMBER SHOULD CARE; THE CTA. Short enough to use live.

## 9. LIKELY OBJECTIONS
The three most likely objections or questions given THIS member's politics and record, each with a concise answer. Use the FAQ file as the baseline when supplied; otherwise answer from verified facts and mark RECOMMENDATION.

## 10. 15–20 MINUTE MEETING PLAN
- `staff` mode default: Opening 1 min; Relationship bridge 0–1 min; Issues 1–3 at ~3–4 min each, ask-first; Discussion and process questions 4–6 min; Closing 30 sec repeating the three asks, then confirm the leave-behind and follow-up contact.
- `member` mode: Opening 1–2; Relationship bridge 1–2; Issues ~3 each; Discussion 3–5; Closing 30 sec.
Suggest which delegation member presents each issue where useful.

## 11. INTELLIGENCE OBJECTIVES
What to learn: willingness to cosponsor and what blocks it; the office's cosponsorship policy and process; preferred legislative vehicle; the Member's concerns; who handles Taiwan/China; supporting information that would help; which argument landed; expected follow-up. In `staff` mode also: who decides, and the realistic timeline.

## 12. FOLLOW-UP PLAN
Tracker template:

| Issue | CTA | Staff Response | Commitment | Concern | Materials Requested | Follow-up Owner | Deadline |

Response categories: YES, LEAN YES, NEEDS INFORMATION, UNDER REVIEW, NEUTRAL, CONCERN, NO. "Sounds supportive" is not a commitment. In `staff` mode, a staffer saying they will recommend it is recorded as LEAN YES or UNDER REVIEW with a follow-up date, not YES.

## 13. VERIFICATION NOTES
Group by VERIFIED / PACKET-DERIVED / INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION / NEEDS RECHECK. Inline links for every verifiable claim. Include `STATUS CHECKED: <date>` and:

RECHECK MORNING OF MEETING:
- bill status
- cosponsor roster
- scheduled markup
- scheduled floor action
- committee membership if necessary
